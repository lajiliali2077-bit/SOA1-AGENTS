# AGENTS.md (Level 3: Finance / Treasury)

Rules for cash position and forecast skills. They override the Finance department rules where they differ.

## Forecast conventions
- A forecast covers 30 days and is given per week (4 full weeks, then the remaining days as a fifth line).
- Start from the real balance of each account at the start date, never from an estimate.
- Include scheduled payments and known incoming payments only. Mark any unconfirmed incoming payment as "expected" and show it on a separate line.
- Give the forecast as three lines per week: incoming, outgoing, closing balance.

## Presentation (override)
- Summaries use thousands of TND with 1 decimal (KTND), for example 125.4 KTND. This overrides the 3-decimal rule of the department for summaries only.
- Detail tables and any figure used to justify a decision keep the full amount in TND with 3 decimals.

## Cash buffer
- The minimum cash buffer is 50000 TND per account group. Flag any week where the closing balance falls below it, with the week and the shortfall.
- A negative closing balance is always flagged first in the answer.

## Limits
- Never give investment advice and never recommend a financial product. Only report figures and trends.
- Never create or modify payment orders from a forecast skill. These skills are read only in practice, even if the department allows write tools.
- Foreign currency flows are converted with the central bank rate of the forecast start date. State that rate once in the answer.
