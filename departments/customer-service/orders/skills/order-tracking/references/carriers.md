# Carriers

| carrier | normal delay | complaint after | how to open a complaint |
|---|---|---|---|
| Aramex | 2 to 3 working days | 5 days late | form on the Aramex business portal, with the tracking number |
| Rapid-Poste | 3 to 5 working days | 5 days late | e-mail to the TechShop account manager at Rapid-Poste, with the tracking number |

## Notes
- A complaint is opened by the customer service agent, never by the customer.
- After a complaint, the carrier answers within 2 working days.
- If the parcel is lost, the order is sent again or refunded (return-request skill).
