# AGENTS.md (Level 3: Customer service / Orders and deliveries)

Rules for order tracking and customer account skills. They override the department rules where they differ.

## Delivery times
- The standard delivery time is 3 working days after the order date.
- An order is late when it is not delivered on the expected delivery date. Give the number of days late.
- After 5 days late, the agent must open a complaint with the carrier (see references/carriers.md of order-tracking) and tell the customer.

## Tracking information
- Give the carrier name, the tracking number and the last tracking event with its date and place.
- Never promise a delivery date. Say "expected" and give the date of the carrier.

## Customer accounts
- Gold customers are served first. Mention the tier at the start of the answer.
- A customer with more than 3 refunds in 90 days must be reported under "To check:".
