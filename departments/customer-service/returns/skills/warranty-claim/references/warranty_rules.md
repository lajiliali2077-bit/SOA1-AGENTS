# Warranty rules

| situation | solution |
|---|---|
| defect inside the warranty, first claim | free repair by the after-sales team |
| same defect a second time inside the warranty | replacement by a new product |
| product cannot be repaired or replaced | refund of the price paid (return-request skill, supervisor approval) |
| warranty over | no free solution: offer a paid repair quote |
| damage caused by the customer (drop, water) | not covered by the warranty |

## Notes
- The warranty starts on the delivery date, not on the order date.
- Clothing has no manufacturer warranty: only the return window applies.
- The after-sales team answers within 5 working days.
